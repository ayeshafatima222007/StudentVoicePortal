Submit a complaint

Choose how you want to submit. Both options give you a tracking reference.

Anonymous complaint

Raise an issue without revealing who you are.

  • No account or login
  • No registration number, batch, section or class timing
  • No identity information of any kind
  • You select a category and a predefined issue; there is no free-text box
  • Meetings cannot be arranged, so you stay anonymous
Submit anonymous complaint

Identified complaint

Share your details so the department can follow up with you.

  • Registration number, batch, section and class timing are collected
  • You write a detailed description in your own words
  • The department can propose a meeting to discuss it with you
