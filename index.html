How it works

1. Submit

Choose anonymous or identified, pick a category and send it. It takes about a minute.

2. Save your reference

Every submission gets a reference such as SVP-8F4K29. Copy it or write it down.

3. Track the outcome

Enter the reference to see the status and the department's response. Identified complaints may also get a meeting.

Complain without revealing who you are

Anonymous complaints need no account. You choose a category and a predefined issue, and only your tracking reference links you to it.
